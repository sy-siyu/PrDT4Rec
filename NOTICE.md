# Third-party attribution

The causal modality convolution is adapted from Decision ConvFormer (MIT),
https://github.com/beanie00/Decision-ConvFormer, commit
3db9b9ecf6885b1ed9c1e7a20d5dd92aa132b1ef. Recommendation tokenization,
likelihoods, preference fitting and trajectory weighting are project adaptations.

The native environment transition adapters reproduce the relevant EasyRL4Rec
(MIT) stopping and feedback conventions, https://github.com/chongminggao/EasyRL4Rec,
commit 95d0acfa55e2150d0775ebd41de0826272edcc94.
Third-party license texts are included under licenses/.

This package contains no original dataset, trained response model, or benchmark
result. Obtain external data and simulators separately under their own terms.
